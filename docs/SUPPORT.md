# Support Policy

## What's included

- Access to the documentation in this `docs/` folder (architecture, data
  schema, methodology, configuration, customisation, deployment, privacy,
  limitations).
- Reasonable email support for installation/setup questions and genuine
  product defects, for 90 days from purchase.
- Bug fixes for defects in the delivered code, at the seller's discretion and
  timeline.

## What's not included

- Custom development, feature additions, or bespoke integrations (available
  separately by arrangement).
- Support for heavily modified copies of the source code.
- Data-analysis consulting or interpretation of your specific business
  results.
- Guaranteed response times or 24/7 support.
- Hosting, deployment, or infrastructure management on your behalf.

## How to get help

Use the contact method listed on the Etsy listing/order page. Include:
1. What you were trying to do
2. What happened instead
3. Your browser and OS
4. Screenshots if relevant (with any personal data redacted)

## Refunds

Refund requests are handled per Etsy's policies for digital downloads and
applicable consumer-protection law in your jurisdiction. Because this is a
digital product delivered instantly, refunds are generally not available
once the files have been downloaded, except where required by law or in
cases of a genuine, unresolved product defect — contact support first so we
can attempt to fix the issue.
